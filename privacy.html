Privacy Quest Log

Your privacy is the ultimate shield. Loot Tracker is built on a "Zero Data" philosophy—meaning your financial secrets stay with you, and only you.

1. No Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or share any of your personal or financial data. In fact, we don't even have a server to send it to. There is no account creation required to start your adventure.

2. Local Storage Only

All your loot, quest logs, and character stats are stored locally on your own device. Your data never leaves your "inventory" (phone or computer).

3. Security & Ownership

Since no account is needed and no data is transmitted, your financial information is as secure as your device itself. You have 100% ownership over your financial quest logs.

4. Contact Us

If you have questions about your data privacy, send a raven (or an email) to support@loottracker.game.
